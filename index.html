Bio

Some stuff about me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia.

Previously, I worked as a public sector data scientist, and I was originally trained as a digital humanist. I still enjoy applying a computational lens to questions in the humanities and a humanist lens to computational questions. I also try my best to build data-driven systems that serve our communities and to think about how to mitigate and avoid the harms that can inadvertently stem from such systems.
